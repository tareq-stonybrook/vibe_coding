    Get help answering a hospital stay denial

    This guide helps you organize an appeal when your insurance denied coverage for a hospital stay because it said the stay was not medically necessary.

    What this guide does

    • Helps you write down the reason given on your denial notice.
    • Shows appeal information for your state and insurance type, only when our team has checked it against an official source.
    • Gives you a checklist of information that is commonly requested.
    • Builds a draft letter you review, edit and download.

    What this guide does not do

    • It does not give legal advice or medical advice.
    • It does not decide whether you are covered.
    • It does not create medical facts or evidence.
    • It does not submit anything. You send your appeal yourself.

    For official help, visit HealthCare.gov (opens in new tab).
