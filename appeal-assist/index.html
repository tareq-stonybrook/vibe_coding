Appeal Assist

    Appeal Assist

    Helping patients understand denials and prepare an appeal.

    Break down your denial notice, be informed on requested documents, and receive a drafted appeal letter to review and submit.

    What it does

    • Reads the details from an example notice file, or lets you type them.
    • Shows appeal rules only when they have been checked against an official source, with the source and date checked.
    • Lists information that is commonly requested.
    • Writes a draft letter for you, using only what you confirmed. Anything missing stays marked for you to add.

    What it does not do

    • Give legal or medical advice.
    • Decide whether you are covered.
    • Create medical facts or evidence.
    • Ask for your name, address, phone or member ID. You add those privately after you download the draft.
    • Submit anything. You send your appeal yourself.
    • Promise an outcome. We are not responsible for whether your appeal is approved or denied.

    For official help, visit HealthCare.gov (opens in new tab).

    Review

    Check it before you download

    Fill in each gap, or leave it for later. Gaps left for later stay marked in the downloaded draft, and the draft is not ready to send until you complete them.

    Send

    Download and send your letter

    Check all details and attach your supporting information. This guide gives no legal or medical advice and does not submit your appeal. You send it yourself.
