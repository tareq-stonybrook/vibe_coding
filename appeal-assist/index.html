    Before you begin: privacy and limits

    Use made-up notices and details only. Files are read in your browser using text matching, not AI. The reader can miss details; you must check them.

    This tool does not decide coverage, provide medical or legal advice, submit appeals, or promise approval. Policy guidance still needs team sign-off. Your progress clears when you reload or close the page.
