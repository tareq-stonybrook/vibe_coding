Appeal Assist

    Inpatient medical necessity denials

    Answer a hospital stay denial, one step at a time.

    Organize what your notice says, see what to gather, and get a draft appeal letter you review and send yourself.

    Prototype. Use synthetic notices only. Do not enter or upload real patient information.

    What it does

    • Reads the details from your notice file, or lets you type them.
    • Shows appeal rules only when our team has checked them against an official source.
    • Lists information that is commonly requested.
    • Writes a draft letter for you, using only what you confirmed.

    What it does not do

    • Give legal or medical advice.
    • Decide whether you are covered.
    • Create medical facts or evidence.
    • Submit anything. You send your appeal yourself.

    For official help, visit HealthCare.gov (opens in new tab).
